01 — Capabilities

One platform for every kind of customer voice.

Text, reviews, surveys, and voice recordings — analyzed together to reveal what your customers actually mean.

Text Feedback Analyzer

Paste reviews or upload CSVs. We extract sentiment, keywords, complaints, feature requests, and praise — with original text beside its analysis.

Voice Feedback Analyzer

Record from microphone or upload audio. Speech-to-text transcribes, then analyzes transcript sentiment, topics, and concerns — with playback.

Customer Intelligence

Interactive dashboards with sentiment distribution, trending topics, common complaints, and satisfaction metrics — built with Recharts.

AI Insight Generator

Business recommendations grounded in real data: what customers love, what frustrates them, requested features, and recommended priorities.

Feedback Explorer

Search by keyword. Filter by sentiment, topic, date, and source. Sort by newest, oldest, or sentiment. Export filtered results to CSV.

Analysis History

Reopen or delete past sessions. Stored locally in this single-browser demo — swap in Supabase for persistent multi-user history.

05 — AI Insight Generator

Recommendations grounded in real feedback.

Generated from the demo dataset. Every recommendation references real mentions — never fabricated quotes or statistics.

What customers love

  • Fast checkout flow — mentioned 318× positively.
  • Intuitive mobile app design — 274× positive.
  • Helpful support agents when reachable — 161×.

Frequently reported problems

  • Shipping delays on standard orders — 412×.
  • App crashes during login — 287×.
  • Slow support response times — 189×.

Most-requested features

  • Apple Pay & Google Pay at checkout — 144×.
  • Dark mode in mobile app — 98×.
  • Order tracking with live updates — 87×.

Potential causes of negative sentiment

  • Carrier handoff delays during peak season correlate with 78% of shipping complaints.
  • Login crashes cluster on older Android builds — likely a session-token regression.
  • Pricing confusion stems from overlap between “Pro” and “Team” tiers.

Recommended priorities

  1. Fix login crash regression — highest negative intensity.
  2. Add Apple/Google Pay — quick win, high request volume.
  3. Rework pricing tiers or add comparison tool.
  4. Reduce support response SLA to < 4h.
